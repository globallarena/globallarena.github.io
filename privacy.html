Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

Globall ("we", "our") is an independent content project that publishes short, automatically generated physics-game videos to its own social media accounts (YouTube channel @globallarena, Instagram @globallarena and TikTok @globall.arena). This policy explains how the Globall uploader application ("the App") handles data.

Who uses the App

The App is an internal tool used only by the Globall project owner to manage Globall's own accounts. It is not offered to the public and does not provide sign-in for other users.

Data the App accesses

  • YouTube (via YouTube API Services): uploading videos to the Globall channel, and reading the channel's own video list and basic statistics (views, likes, comments counts) through the YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API.
  • Instagram (via Meta's Instagram API): publishing Reels to the Globall Instagram account and reading that account's own media insights.

The App does not access, collect or store personal data of viewers or any other YouTube or Instagram users.

How data is used and stored

Statistics of Globall's own videos are used only to decide which game formats to produce more often. Access tokens are stored as encrypted secrets in the project's private automation environment and are never shared. We do not sell, rent or share any data with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

YouTube API Services

The App uses YouTube API Services. By using the App you are also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Access granted to the App can be revoked at any time from the Google security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.

The App's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Data retention and deletion

Video statistics are kept only as aggregated numbers for content planning. Stored tokens and statistics can be deleted at any time on request. To request deletion, contact us at the address below.

Children

The App does not knowingly collect any information from children or any other individuals.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
